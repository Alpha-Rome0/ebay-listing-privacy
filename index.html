Privacy Policy

eBay Listing Workspace

Effective date: November 8, 2026

About this workspace

eBay Listing Workspace is a private tool used by its operator to research, prepare, and manage the operator's eBay listings. It is not a public service and does not provide accounts for other users.

Information handled

The workspace handles seller-provided inventory details and photos, public listing information returned by eBay, and eBay API credentials needed to access the seller's account. These files and credentials are kept in the operator's local workspace. Credentials are excluded from source control.

The workspace does not retrieve buyer or order data. Its eBay account deletion notification endpoint verifies incoming notifications and acknowledges them without saving or logging their payloads.

How information is used and shared

Seller-provided listing information is used to prepare listings and is sent to eBay only when the operator uses eBay APIs or eBay's seller tools. Public eBay listing information is used for market research. The workspace does not sell personal information or share it with advertising services.

Storage and retention

Inventory files, photos, and local API credentials remain on the operator's device until the operator removes them. The workspace does not maintain a separate hosted database of seller or buyer information. If this page is published through GitHub Pages, GitHub may process page-visit data under its own privacy terms.

Choices and deletion

The operator can remove local workspace files and revoke the application's access from the eBay account. The notification endpoint acknowledges eBay account-deletion requests but does not retain their contents.
